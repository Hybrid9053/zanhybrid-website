Who We Are

About ZanHybrid

ZanHybrid is a technology business based in Zanzibar, working with hotels, guesthouses, restaurants, tour operators, shops, schools, NGOs and offices across Zanzibar and Tanzania.

Many small and medium businesses here either have no proper online presence, or have technology set up in a way that doesn't actually support how the business runs day to day - a website nobody can update, Wi-Fi that keeps dropping, email that looks unprofessional. ZanHybrid exists to fix that, practically and without overselling.

We work across website development, IT support, networking, cloud & email, cybersecurity and IT consultancy - so a business can come to one partner instead of juggling several vendors.

Our approach is straightforward: understand what the business actually needs, recommend only what will help, and stay reachable afterwards - by WhatsApp, phone, or email - for ongoing support.
