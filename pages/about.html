Our Story

Redefining Technology Partnerships in Africa

ZanHybrid was founded with a singular mission: to make enterprise-grade technology accessible to businesses of every size, anywhere in the world. Starting from the vibrant tech ecosystem of Tanzania, we've grown into a comprehensive technology partner trusted by organizations across Africa and beyond.

We believe that technology should be an enabler, not a barrier. Our hybrid approach — combining deep technical expertise with genuine business understanding — ensures that every solution we deliver creates real, measurable value.

From a small startup to a team of 50+ certified professionals, our journey has been defined by an unwavering commitment to excellence, innovation, and the success of our clients.

Our Journey

A Decade of Growth

2014
Founded in Dar es Salaam

ZanHybrid was established with a 3-person team and a bold vision to democratize enterprise IT in Africa.

2016
Cloud Services Launch

Expanded into cloud services, becoming one of the first AWS-certified partners in Tanzania.

2018
Cyber Security Division

Launched dedicated cybersecurity practice following growing demand for enterprise security solutions.

2021
Regional Expansion

Expanded operations to Kenya, Uganda and Rwanda, serving 300+ clients across East Africa.

2024
500+ Clients Milestone

Celebrated serving over 500 clients and 50+ certified professionals. Recognized as Top ICT Company in the region.
