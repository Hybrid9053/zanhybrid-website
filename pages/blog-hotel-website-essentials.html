5 Things Every Zanzibar Hotel Should Have on Its Website

← Back to Blog

Most guests decide whether to book, or move on to the next hotel, within the first few seconds on your website. Here's what actually matters.

1. Clear room and rate information

Guests want to know what they're booking and roughly what it costs before they contact you. Even without live booking, clear room descriptions and pricing build trust.

2. A WhatsApp booking button

Many guests, especially regional travelers, prefer messaging over email or forms. A visible WhatsApp button removes friction between "interested" and "booked."

3. Real photos

Generic stock photos hurt more than they help. Photos of the actual rooms, grounds, and views set honest expectations and build confidence.

4. Location and Google Maps

Especially in Zanzibar, exact location matters for guests planning transport. An embedded map removes back-and-forth questions.

5. Fast loading on mobile

Most guests are browsing from a phone, often on slower connections while traveling. A heavy, slow site loses bookings before it ever loads.
